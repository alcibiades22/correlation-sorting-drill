Correlation or Causal Claim?

A sorting drill for PHIL 200 — Critical Thinking

Two kinds of statements look similar but say very different things.

A correlation describes a pattern — two things that tend to go together. It says nothing about why.
A causal claim goes further — it says one thing brings about, produces, or is responsible for the other.

Your job: read each statement and sort it. Watch for the signal words.

Correlation signals

  • associated with
  • tend to
  • linked to
  • go together
  • correlated
  • higher rates of
  • predicts

Causal signals

  • causes
  • leads to
  • makes
  • produces
  • results in
  • because
  • is responsible for
